Step inside the shop

An honest London-Road off-licence and convenience store. We’ve laid the counter out the way you’d walk it — the cellar first, then the everyday. No fuss, no fanfare. Just a useful shop on a useful corner.

№ I

The Cellar

  • Wine — red, white & rosé by the bottle
  • Sparkling & Prosecco weekend
  • Beer & cider — singles & multipacks chilled
  • Craft & world beers rotating
  • Spirits — whisky, vodka, gin, rum full range
  • Liqueurs & mixers the basics
№ II

The Counter

  • Cigarettes & rolling tobacco behind the gantry
  • Vape pods & refills selected
  • Newspapers & magazines morning
  • National Lottery & Scratchcards weekly draws
  • Top-ups, e-cards & PayPoint where available
№ III

The Everyday

  • Bread, milk & eggs the staples
  • Tea, coffee & sugar kitchen-cupboard
  • Tinned & dry goods cupboard fillers
  • Snacks, crisps & nuts aisle two
  • Soft drinks & juices chilled
  • Confectionery — chocolate & sweets by the till

A neighbourhood shop changes with the week — pop in to see today’s counter. If we haven’t got it on the shelf, the chances are we’ll point you to whoever does, two doors down.

Find us on the London Road

We’re part of the parade running up out of town — one of the small shops you pass every day until the day you need one. That’s the day to remember the number 102.
